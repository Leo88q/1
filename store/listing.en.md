## Full description

Ampergrad sits inside a giant natural battery mountain. Light here is not a
metaphor — the city mines it, stores it in veins and spends it. You are the last
apprentice of the Key Guild, forgotten on the shutdown list: bring the current
back to the districts, tier by tier.

**Simple rules.** The board is filled with conduit pieces: elbows, straights,
tees and fixed crossings. Tap rotates a piece clockwise, press-and-hold rotates
it counter-clockwise. Crossings never turn. A district is sealed when every cell
is connected to the power bus.

**A session is 1–3 minutes.** One district takes a couple of minutes; there are
75 of them across five tiers — First Contact Plateau, Mercury Tier, Terminal
Tier, the Old Diode and the Melter. Plus a daily challenge: the same district for
everyone, ranked by move count.

**Play without a wallet, without a token.** Gameplay is fully offline. A wallet
is only needed to claim rewards: guild seals and registry pages are issued as
compressed NFTs (cNFTs) on Solana. The player signs no transactions and pays no
fees — the project server performs the mint.

**Inside:**
- 75 districts, each with a dispatcher-style name and code (P-01 … Ya-15);
- the city registry: 60 short lore entries unlocked by playing;
- a daily challenge with streak tracking;
- a cosmetics shop — appearance only: palettes, tier themes, power sources,
  seals. Nothing that affects play. Some items are unlocked with **in-game
  coins** earned by clearing districts (20 plus 10 per star, plus 20 for the
  daily challenge); coins are never sold, bought or cashed out and live only
  on the device. On-chain rewards are minted solely for server-verified solves.

**About rewards, honestly:** this is not "earn a token". Rewards are collectible
achievements; they are not sold and promise no income. A seasonal prize pool, if
announced, is funded from project revenue and described in the season rules.
